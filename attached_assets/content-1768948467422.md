##### Topics covered in this course

## **What’s Covered in This Course**

This course is designed to give you real-world, practical training in the craft of product management. It’s structured around three pillars: the**Mindsets**that shape how you think, the**Processes**that guide your actions, and the**Tools**that help you execute with confidence.

### **Topic \#1: The Product Function and the Role of a Product Manager**

- **Mindset:**PMs are outcome owners, not task managers. Your role is to solve problems, not to build features.

- **Process:**Understanding the core responsibilities of a PM and how they vary across companies.

- **Tools:**Product org charts, team collaboration workflows, stakeholder maps.


* * *

### **Topic \#2: The End-to-End Product Management Process**

- **Mindset:**Product management is a repeatable system—not chaos.

- **Process:**Step-by-step walkthrough from discovery to delivery to iteration.

- **Tools:**Product lifecycle maps, feedback loops, Agile vs. Waterfall models.


* * *

### **Topic \#3: How to Manage Product Ideas**

- **Mindset:**Not all ideas are good ideas. Learn to detach from feature bias.

- **Process:**Capturing, triaging, and organizing raw ideas for evaluation.

- **Tools:**Idea management boards, templates for idea review and scoring.


* * *

### **Topic \#4: How to Set Up Objectives and Metrics for Your Product**

- **Mindset:**What gets measured gets managed. Goals should tie to business outcomes.

- **Process:**Defining product objectives, success metrics, and tracking progress.

- **Tools:**OKRs, North Star metrics, KPI dashboards, metric trees.


* * *

### **Topic \#5: How to Map Out Product Assumptions**

- **Mindset:**Assumptions are risks in disguise. You can’t validate what you haven’t surfaced.

- **Process:**Identifying and categorizing assumptions to prioritize validation.

- **Tools:**Assumption mapping canvas, risk grids, validation checklist.


* * *

### **Topic \#6: Using Customer Research to Validate Customer Problems**

- **Mindset:**Fall in love with the problem, not the solution.

- **Process:**Running effective user interviews and problem discovery research.

- **Tools:**Interview scripts, empathy maps, JTBD frameworks.


* * *

### **Topic \#7: Doing Market Research for Your Product**

- **Mindset:**You don’t build in a vacuum—your product exists in a market.

- **Process:**Analyzing competitors, industry trends, and market sizing.

- **Tools:**SWOT analysis, TAM/SAM/SOM models, competitor feature matrices.


* * *

### **Topic \#8: How to Use MVPs to Validate Your Solution**

- **Mindset:**You’re not trying to prove you’re right—you’re trying to learn fast.

- **Process:**Designing experiments and MVPs to de-risk your product.

- **Tools:**MVP experiment design templates, learning backlogs, validation scorecards.


* * *

### **Topic \#9: Turning Research Insights Into Product Features**

- **Mindset:**Insights are only valuable if they lead to action.

- **Process:**Translating qualitative and quantitative data into product opportunities.

- **Tools:**Insight-to-feature mapping templates, customer journey maps.


* * *

### **Topic \#10: Prioritisation Methods for Product Managers**

- **Mindset:**Saying no is just as important as saying yes.

- **Process:**Comparing trade-offs and sequencing work based on impact.

- **Tools:**RICE, MoSCoW, Impact-Effort matrices, GEM model.


* * *

### **Topic \#11: Design for Product Managers**

- **Mindset:**Good design is about solving the right problem simply.

- **Process:**Collaborating with designers and giving useful feedback.

- **Tools:**Wireframes, user flows, design review checklists.


* * *

### **Topic \#12: Coming Up With a Great Product Strategy**

- **Mindset:**Strategy is about focus and sequencing, not grand vision statements.

- **Process:**Aligning business goals with customer needs through strategic bets.

- **Tools:**Strategy templates, vision ladders, opportunity solution trees.


* * *

### **Topic \#13: Creating a Kick-Ass Product Roadmap**

- **Mindset:**Roadmaps are communication tools, not commitments.

- **Process:**Translating strategy into themes and releases over time.

- **Tools:**Roadmapping tools, theme-based roadmap templates, release plans.


* * *

### **Topic \#14: Finding Product-Market Fit**

- **Mindset:**PMF is not a milestone, it’s a moving target.

- **Process:**Detecting signals of PMF and iterating your way toward it.

- **Tools:**PMF surveys, retention curves, engagement metrics, NPS tracking.


* * *

### **Topic \#15: Technology Fundamentals for Product Managers**

- **Mindset:**You don’t need to code—but you do need to speak the language.

- **Process:**Understanding the tech stack and how products are architected.

- **Tools:**API documentation, data flow diagrams, common tech stacks.


* * *

### **Topic \#16: Leading a Tech Team as a Product Manager**

- **Mindset:**Influence without authority starts with trust and clarity.

- **Process:**Running product rituals and creating alignment across teams.

- **Tools:**Sprint planning templates, standup formats, team charter examples.


* * *

### **Topic \#17: Practical Agile for Product Managers**

- **Mindset:**Agile is not a religion—it’s a toolbox.

- **Process:**Applying Agile methods to get learning and value faster.

- **Tools:**Scrum boards, backlog grooming templates, sprint review formats.
